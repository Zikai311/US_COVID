## Layout & conventions

- `src/*.py` are flat modules, **not** a package — import as `from sir import simulate_sir` (notebooks do `sys.path.insert(0, '../src')`; never `import src.sir`).
- `data/` holds real NYT COVID CSVs (`nyt_us_national.csv`, `nyt_us_states.csv`) used by `data_loader.py` and notebooks 06–08, plus aviation data: `t100_market_2020_2023.csv.gz` (raw BTS T-100 Market, US carriers, 852,845 rows, monthly, downloaded from transtats.bts.gov 2026-08-05 — the authoritative source for everything below; it supersedes the hard-coded `metapopulation.BTS_MONTHLY_SCALE` which only covered 2020-01→2022-04 and was approximate, max |Δ|≈0.30 vs T-100; the module dict is still there until the metapopulation rework), `tsa_checkpoint_daily.csv` (raw TSA checkpoint throughput, daily, 2019-01-01→2023-12-31, fetched from tsa.gov 2026-08-05), `bts_state_pairs_monthly.csv` (**state→state** monthly passengers, 63,721 rows, 56×56 jurisdictions — the `W_ij` input), `bts_state_monthly.csv` (per-state monthly series: single time column `month` as `YYYY-MM`, plus `state`, `outbound`, `inbound`, `net` = inbound−outbound; missing months = zero traffic, e.g. DE 2022-08) and `bts_state_jan2020.csv` (per-state Jan-2020 outbound baseline, state-constant — the denominator for the model's per-state `miu(t)`; `scale_out` is deliberately **not** stored, the model computes its own mobility factor). **Road data (added 2026-08-06, candidate inputs for a future multimodal mobility factor — the model has NOT been changed):** `fhwa_vmt_national_monthly.csv` (national monthly vehicle-miles, All Systems, 2019-01→2024-12, millions; source FHWA Traffic Volume Trends) and `fhwa_vmt_state_monthly.csv` (per-state monthly VMT, 51 states × 2020-01→2024-12, with prior-year-same-month VMT and % change; parsed from the 60 monthly TVT files by `scripts/fetch_fhwa_vmt.py` — rerun `venv/bin/python scripts/fetch_fhwa_vmt.py` to re-download/re-parse; raw xls/xlsx cached in `data/.tvt_tmp/`). Key 2020 contrast: April-2020 air collapsed to ≈5 % of Jan-2020 while car VMT fell only to ≈64 % — a pure-air mobility scale overstates the mobility collapse ~2×; a weighted `s_i(t)` (air+car) is the documented next step. Published long-distance mode shares (BTS/NHTS 2017): personal vehicle ≈ 89 % of trips / ≈ 75 % of person-miles, air ≈ 8 % of trips / ≈ 19 % of person-miles → air ≈ 10–20 % of inter-state person-travel (μ₀-rescale candidate). **NPI/behaviour data (added 2026-08-06, drive the time-varying β(t) of notebook 08 §8.13):** `google_cmr_state_daily.csv` + `google_cmr_state_monthly.csv` (Google Community Mobility Reports, US state-level daily % change vs Jan-2020 baseline, 2020-02-15→2022-10, categories retail/grocery/parks/transit/workplaces/residential; row `code='US'` = national fallback; territories absent) and `oxcgrt_state_daily.csv` + `oxcgrt_state_monthly.csv` (Oxford stringency index 0–100, 50 states, no DC) — fetched by `scripts/fetch_mobility_beta_data.py` (CMR zip cached in `data/.cmr_tmp/`). **Vaccination data (added 2026-08-06, planned SEIRV susceptible-pool term, formulae_sheet §6 decision 11 — model NOT yet changed):** `cdc_vax_state_daily.csv` + `cdc_vax_state_monthly.csv` (CDC "COVID-19 Vaccinations in the United States, County" `8xkx-amqh`, aggregated state×date by `scripts/fetch_vaccination_data.py`: `series_complete`/`dose1`/`boosters` cumulative people + monthly `series_complete_flow` = the v_i(t) input; 56 jurisdictions, 2020-12-13→2023-05-10; national fully-vaccinated ≈ 42 % by 2021-06, 51 % by 2021-09, 61 % by 2021-12). Note: data.bts.gov's "COVID-19 Passenger Impact" (Passengers by Month) and "Trips by Distance with County Data" datasets are empty placeholders / one-week samples — the real data sits behind bts.gov (bot-blocked) or the registration-gated MDC platform; the T-100/TSA/VMT set above is the accessible substitute. The derived **national** scale CSVs (`bts_aviation_monthly.csv`, `bts_aviation_daily.csv`) were **removed** (2026-08-05): the project models state-level mobility, and the national series is only used for the §7.5 figures in notebook 07, where it is computed in-memory from the raw files. Jurisdiction mapping quirks (see notebook 07 §7.6): DCA is re-attributed to DC (T-100 codes it VA; IAD→VA, BWI→MD); T-100's single Pacific code 'TT' is split by airport into GU (GUM,UAM), MP (SPN,TIQ,ROP), AS (PPG); AWK/MDY dropped. All 56 NYT jurisdictions have data. `data_loader.py` has `load_tsa_daily()` / `load_bts_state_pairs()` / `load_bts_state_monthly()` / `load_bts_state_jan2020()`; the state-level derived CSVs are regenerated by notebook 07 §7.6. `data_loader.py` resolves `data/` relative to the repo root.
- Figures are saved **directly into `assets/`** (e.g. `assets/fig37_national_fit.png`, dpi=300), continuing the `figNN_<name>` numbering (latest: fig81, notebook 08 §8.16). `assets/figures`, `gifs/`, `videos/` subdirs are unused/empty; the notebooks write to `assets/` root.
- `fused_metapopulation.py` (notebook 08): `FusedMetapopulationSEIR` — 56 jurisdictions (50 states + DC + 5 territories), degree-class SEIR (truncated power law, ⟨k⟩=6, α=2.659, 198 classes) × time-varying gravity mobility: `W_ij(t) = s_i(t)·μ0_i·W^grav_ij`; `calibrate_mobility()` (excludes intra-state self-flows; `pair_level=True` returns per-pair μ0_ij/s_ij(t) — the **saturated data-replay variant**, used by appendix A §A.9 and notebook 08 §8.12; `FusedMetapopulationSEIR` accepts `W_tensor=..., months=...` for that mode), `build_gravity_kernel(b_N, b_d)` (data-calibrated exponents from `fit_gravity_exponents` PPML: b_N≈1.00, b_d≈0.55), `fit_beta_from_growth()` (per-state β, slope-invariant, R₀ cap), **time-varying β: `beta_scale=(n, T)` kwarg (β_i(t) = β0_i·scale[i,month(t)]) + `cmr_beta_scale()` / `stringency_beta_scale()` builders (notebook 08 §8.13)**, **SEIRV vaccination: `vac_flow=(n,T)` + `vac_efficacy` + `V0_dict` + `waning` + `R_init_dict` (prior-infection immunity) kwargs → 5-compartment S-E-I-R-V (55,440 ODEs); `vac_flow=None` = bit-identical 4-compartment SEIR (notebook 08 §8.14)**, `simulate()` (RK45, t_eval; conservation is global, per-state totals move with travellers); two-regime β(t) switch + fitted π₀ (nb08 §8.16, appendix B §B.6). θ = infectious-stub fraction `Σk·I/N⟨k⟩` (the P-weighted form is subcritical — do not "fix" it back).
- `metapopulation.py` (legacy, notebook 07): `MetapopulationSIR` simulates 51 states (50+DC); `fit_state_betas()` requires `simulate()` to be run first; gravity matrix is row-normalized, mobility scaled via its own `BTS_MONTHLY_SCALE` dict (approximate; superseded by T-100).
